SECTION ADECK 02/06

The Problem Space

Why equipment tracking breaks down without a system behind it.

  • ObservationManual tracking. Corporate equipment sign-out is often a spreadsheet or a sticky note — easy to lose, easy to forget.
  • ObservationNo enforcement. Quota limits and duration caps are hard to enforce by hand, so they quietly get ignored.
  • ObservationSilent conflicts. Overlapping reservations on the same item cause double-bookings nobody catches until pickup day.
  • ObservationMaintenance gaps. Equipment down for service isn't visibly flagged, so it still gets requested.

SECTION BDECK 03/06

The Approach

A small, disciplined stack — no more moving parts than the problem needs.

  • StackNode.js + Express API, paired with a hand-built vanilla HTML/CSS/JS front end styled as a physical inventory ledger.
  • ValidationA strict request gauntlet — date bounds, duration caps, double-booking checks, and quota control run on every reservation.
  • TestingJest + Supertest coverage for edge cases, invalid input, and security constraints, not just the happy path.
